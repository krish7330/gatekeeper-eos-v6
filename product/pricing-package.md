## 1. Value Proposition (What You're Selling)

### The Problem You Solve

Indian SMBs lose customers every day because:
- **Slow replies** — customers wait hours for answers on WhatsApp
- **Missed leads** — after-hours messages go unanswered
- **Inconsistent info** — different staff give different answers
- **No booking system** — customers have to call to book appointments
- **No data** — no visibility into conversations or lead sources

### Your Solution

A 24/7 WhatsApp chatbot that:
- ✅ Replies instantly to every message
- ✅ Answers FAQs about pricing, services, and hours
- ✅ Books appointments automatically
- ✅ Captures leads into a structured database
- ✅ Works in English, Hindi, and Telugu
- ✅ Costs less than one hour of an employee's time per day

### ROI for the Client

| Metric | Without Bot | With Bot |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Response time | 2-6 hours | < 5 seconds |
| After-hours leads | Lost | Captured |
| FAQ handling | Staff time | Automated |
| Booking process | Phone call needed | WhatsApp auto-booking |
| Lead tracking | None | Structured database |
| Monthly cost | ₹15,000-25,000 (staff) | ₹1,999-5,999 (bot) |

## 4. Setup Process (What the Client Gets)

### Phase 1: Onboarding (Day 1)

| Step | What Happens | Who Does It |
|------|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | Sign up and choose a plan | Client |
| 2 | Pay setup fee | Client |
| 3 | Provide Twilio + WhatsApp number | Client |
| 4 | Provide Google Account for Sheets | Client |
| 5 | Share FAQ answers + reply templates | Client |
| 6 | You configure the workflow | You |
| 7 | Test the bot in sandbox mode | Both |
| 8 | Submit for WhatsApp production approval | You |
| 9 | Go live!⏱ (sandbox in 1hr, production in 3-7 days) | You |

### Phase 2: First Week

- **You monitor** all conversations for quality
- **You tweak** reply templates based on real interactions
- **Client provides** any missing FAQ answers
- **Handover** — client gets access to Google Sheets dashboard

### Phase 3: Ongoing

- **You handle** technical maintenance (n8n, Twilio, Google Sheets)
- **Client handles** updating FAQs and reply templates
- **Weekly summary** sent to client via WhatsApp
- **Monthly review** call (Premium tier only)

## 6. Pricing at a Glance (Cheat Sheet)

> ¹ **Soft limits**: These are guidelines, not hard caps. Message volume is reviewed monthly. If consistently exceeded, we'll discuss adjusting your plan. The n8n workflow does not automatically enforce these limits, which keeps your bot running without interruption. I'll monitor manually and proactively suggest a tier change if needed.

### 📋 Hosting & Infrastructure Note

The chatbot runs on an n8n automation server. If you're self-hosting (recommended), the infrastructure cost is ₹0-₹500/month depending on your setup. If using n8n cloud, it starts at ~₹1,700/month ($20). This is included in the pricing for Premium tier only; Starter and Business tiers are priced assuming self-hosted infrastructure.

### 📊 Google Sheets Capacity

Google Sheets has a 10 million cell limit. For most Indian SMBs, this provides 6-12 months of conversation history. I'll set up quarterly data archiving to keep your sheet lean and fast.

## 7. Sales Objections — How to Handle Them

| Objection | Your Response |
|-----------|--------------|
| *"It's too expensive"* | "It costs less than one hour of a staff member's time per day. And it works 24/7. How many leads are you losing after hours?" |
| *"My customers prefer talking to a human"* | "The bot handles the first 3 questions. If they need a human, the system flags the conversation. You still can step in anytime — but now you only talk to the ones who actually need you." |
| *"I don't have time to set it up"* | "I handle the entire setup. You just answer 5 questions about your business. The rest is on me." |
| *"What about Meta fees?"* | "Meta charges ~₹0.11-0.86 per conversation. For a small business doing 500 conversations/month, that's ₹55-430 extra. I'll show you the exact cost before you commit." |
| *"Can I try it first?"* | "Absolutely. I can set up a 7-day free trial. You only pay if you want to keep it after that." |
| *"What if it breaks?"* | "You get real-time error alerts on Telegram. If something goes wrong, I'll know before you do, and I'll fix it within hours." |
