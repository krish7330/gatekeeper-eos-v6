# Alance WhatsApp Bot — Sales Scripts & Outreach Templates

> **Target:** Indian SMBs (small shops, clinics, real estate, coaching centers, service providers)  
> **Channel:** WhatsApp, Email, In-person  
> **Goal:** Book a demo → Free trial → Paid client

---

## 1. Lead Source Templates

### 🗺️ Google Maps Outreach

**Target:** Local businesses with no website or outdated contact info

> "Hi [Name], I noticed [Business Name] on Google Maps. I see you get quite a few customer inquiries. I built a simple WhatsApp bot that automatically replies with your services, pricing, and hours — so you never miss a customer even when you're busy. Costs less than ₹2,000/month. Interested in seeing a 2-minute demo?"

### 📸 Instagram DM Outreach

**Target:** Businesses that complain about "late replies" or "missed messages" in comments

> "Hey [Name]! Saw your post — dealing with late replies is frustrating. I actually built a tool that solves exactly this: an auto-reply WhatsApp bot for businesses. It answers FAQs, books appointments, and captures leads automatically. No technical skills needed. Want me to show you how it works?"

### 👥 Facebook Group Post

**Target:** Small business groups (e.g., "Small Business India," "Mumbai Entrepreneurs")

> "I built a WhatsApp bot for my own business that automatically replies to customers with pricing, hours, and services — and it even books appointments. It's been running for months with zero issues. Happy to share a demo with anyone here who's tired of losing leads after hours. DM me if interested."

### 🏪 In-Person Pitch

**Target:** Local shops, clinics, service centers

> "Hi, I run a service that helps businesses like yours respond to customer messages automatically on WhatsApp. When a customer messages you after hours, the bot replies instantly with your pricing, services, and booking link. It's like having a receptionist available 24/7 for less than ₹100/day. Can I show you a quick demo?"

### 📞 Referral Request

**Target:** Existing clients

> "Hey [Client], glad the bot is working well for you! I'm looking for more businesses like yours who could benefit from this. If you know anyone who struggles with WhatsApp inquiries, I'll give you ₹500 for every referral that signs up. Just send them my way!"

---

> ⚠️ **Anti-spam warning:** Sending too many WhatsApp messages to someone who hasn't opted in can get your own number flagged or blocked by Meta. Keep the WhatsApp sequence to **3 messages max**. If they don't respond, switch to email or move on.

## 3. Demo Call Script

### Opening (30 seconds)

> "Thanks for taking the time to see this. I'm going to show you a WhatsApp bot that I built. It takes me about an hour to set up for any business. After that, it runs automatically.
>
> Here's what it does: When a customer sends a message to your WhatsApp number, the bot reads it, figures out what they want, and replies instantly with the right answer. It can book appointments, share pricing, answer FAQs — all without you doing anything.
>
> Let me show you how it works."

### Demo Walkthrough (5 minutes)

1. Send a message to the demo number
2. Show instant reply with pricing/services
3. Show booking flow (request date/time → confirmation)
4. Show the Google Sheets dashboard (messages, leads, metrics)
5. Show error alerts (Telegram notification)

### Closing (2 minutes)

> "So here's what I'm offering:
>
> - **Starter:** ₹1,999/month — basic auto-replies and FAQ
> - **Business:** ₹3,999/month — full booking + lead capture
> - **Premium:** ₹5,999/month — multi-language + dedicated support
>
> All plans include a 7-day free trial. I set everything up for you.
>
> Which plan sounds like the best fit for [Business Name]?"

## 7. Daily Sales Routine

### Morning (30 min)

1. Send 10 new outreach messages (Google Maps, Instagram, Facebook)
2. Follow up with 5 warm leads
3. Check trial clients — are they using the bot?

### Afternoon (30 min)

1. Handle any issues for active clients
2. Prepare one demo setup
3. Send one case study/follow-up to cold leads

### Evening (15 min)

1. Check errors in Telegram alerts
2. Update lead tracking sheet
3. Set tomorrow's priority target
